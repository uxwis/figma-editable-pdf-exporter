# Editable PDF Exporter

## Tagline

Export Figma frames to PDF with selectable, editable text.

## Description

Turn your Figma designs into multi-page PDFs with selectable, searchable, and editable text.

Editable PDF Exporter exports every top-level frame on the current page as a PDF page, ordered by its position on the canvas. Export a PDF directly, or create a handoff package for your collaborators.

• Keep supported Chinese, English, and mixed-style text editable.
• Export multiple frames into one PDF.
• Create a ZIP with the PDF, font list, compatibility notes, and editing instructions.
• Process everything locally, without uploading your designs or fonts.

How to use

1. Open the Figma page you want to export.
2. Run Editable PDF Exporter.
3. Click “导出 PDF” to export a PDF, or “交接包” to download a handoff package.

Text and fonts

Supported text remains editable. Text with complex effects, missing fonts, or unsupported formatting is converted to outlines to preserve its appearance; outlined text cannot be edited or searched as text. Your original Figma design is unchanged.

Fonts are embedded when accessible and permitted; otherwise, the PDF references installed system fonts. Referenced fonts must be installed on the recipient’s computer to avoid substitution. Editing in Adobe Acrobat may require the same font version locally, even when fonts are embedded. Font files are not included in the handoff package.

The current plugin interface is in Chinese.
